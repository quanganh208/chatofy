Chatofy needs your microphone

This is what lets Chatofy translate what you say into the meeting. Translating what the others say does not need it.

Chrome will ask once, and the answer is remembered for Chatofy — not for any one meeting. The microphone is only opened while a capture is running.

Chrome is remembering an earlier refusal, so it will not ask again. Paste chrome://settings/content/microphone into the address bar, find Chatofy under the blocked list, and remove it. Then reopen this page.
