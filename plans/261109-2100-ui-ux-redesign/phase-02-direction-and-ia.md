# Phase 2: Direction and IA decision (Gate A)

## Steps

1. From phase 1, write the 3 core journeys (translate, conference, history) as step
   lists with friction points and the proposed fix.
2. Propose IA: sidebar order and labels, page titles, empty-state targets, where
   settings live, shortcut discoverability.
3. For `/translate` and `/conference`, produce two layout options as screenshots
   (throwaway branch or open-design prototype), each stating accent and surface count.
4. Study product patterns (Vercel Chatbot/AI Elements, Vexa, shadcn-admin): licence,
   last commit, one relevant component; record port/skip.
   Beauty brief: collect 2-3 owner-named references, extract what makes them work
   (hierarchy, spacing, type, one signature detail) and translate it into our tokens.
   Each layout option states its one memorable detail per screen.
5. Recommendation plus the six open questions from `plan.md`.

## Output

`plans/reports/ui-ux-redesign-261109-direction.md`. Stop and wait for the owner:
phases 3-6 are unscoped until Gate A is answered.
