## Checked and clean

- Declared configuration uses CPU/fp32 for both engines and matching thread settings, including ZeroTTS’s codec.
- WER and CER both normalize references and hypotheses symmetrically; diacritics survive, and numbers are not silently expanded.
- Whole-sentence timing excludes model loading and follows an untimed same-voice warm-up; pooled RTF uses total processing time divided by total audio duration.
- Streaming timing includes lazy generator execution and checks delivery deficits before crediting each arriving chunk.
- The paired bootstrap correctly resamples matched sentences and aggregates edit counts rather than averaging sentence WERs.

## Verdict on the verdict

**Does not survive as stated.** Continuing to HOLD is reasonable, but the evidence does not establish that naturalness is the only remaining decision input. The intelligibility advantage is conditional on selected voices, constructed inputs and a seed despite substantial measured sensitivity; the deployed clause-split baseline is neither scored nor tested for gapless playback end to end. ZeroTTS may retain an average intelligibility advantage, and VieNeu has lower observed pooled RTF, but the report’s certainty and swap condition exceed what these measurements establish.
