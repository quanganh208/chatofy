Speaker-ID channel recorder

Records two tracks from one microphone at the same moment: the production channel, and a DSP-off control. Phase 7 of the speaker-attribution gate.

1 · Setup

The retention date is required before the microphone opens. It covers the WAVs, this page's downloads, and git history — the turn log is a tracked file, and once pushed it cannot be retracted. The language selector applies to every turn recorded from now on; change it mid-session when the conversation switches.

2 · Verify the control track is genuinely raw

Some platforms apply processing below the browser, which would appear on both tracks and make the control useless. Say something loud, then something quiet, for about eight seconds. AGC pulls the quiet part back up on the processed track and leaves the control alone.
